# Printed "Program at a glance" for many parallel sessions

Research for #97 (map #94). Question: how do real printed programs lay out the at-a-glance grid when 6–20+ rooms run at once, and which layout should our Word / IDML / xlsx export pick for a given number of parallel rooms?

## Answer in one line

Rooms-as-columns works up to about 8 rooms on a page and about 14 across a two-page spread or an A3 fold-out. Beyond that, every large meeting surveyed either **transposes** the grid (rooms/symposia as rows, coarse time blocks as columns) or **drops it** for lists ordered by time block. Grid cells hold only a short label (code or short title), never individual talks.

### Patterns

1. **Room-columns ceiling.** Rooms as columns appear up to 11 rooms on Letter landscape (ISMB, with acronym-only cells). 22 rooms took a 28-inch custom sheet (CHI). Above that, nobody surveyed prints rooms as columns.
2. **Transposition is the large-meeting default.** E-MRS (23) and TMS (~77) put symposia/rooms as rows and coarse time blocks (half-days, session blocks) as columns. Rows extend down the page, and a tall list is far easier to paginate than a wide grid.
3. **Dropping the grid** (ACS, EGU, MRS, TMS 2014) happens once a meeting has dozens of rooms, or when it pushes attendees to an app.
4. **Cells hold a label, never talks.** Codes (E-MRS, PVSC, CHI courses), track acronyms (ISMB) or type + title (CHI). ISMB's talk-level grid needed 4.4 pt type, which is a cautionary example.
5. **Grid → detail** goes by session code or track name in most cases. Only TMS 2014 prints page numbers, and it does so in a list, not a grid.
6. **Colour** encodes track/cluster/area (E-MRS cluster bands, PVSC areas, ISMB tracks, TMS topic bands), always with a legend or band label, so the grid survives black-and-white printing.
7. **Landscape grid pages inside a portrait book** are normal (ISMB, E-MRS spread, EGU schedule).
8. **Full-width bands** for breaks, plenaries and keynotes appear in every room-column grid (ISMB, CHI, ANR).
9. **Room names** are kept whole when short (E-MRS: CASSIN, LONDRES 2) and abbreviated when long (PVSC "Blrm D", TMS 2014 "SDCC"). CHI groups columns by building.
10. **Page size** follows the region: US meetings use Letter; EU meetings use A4, with A4 spreads for big grids. Pocket formats also exist (PVSC 4 × 9 in). Parallel load drives the _grid page_ format (landscape, spread, fold-out), not the book format.

## Capacity arithmetic (derived, not sourced)

Usable width assumes 15 mm margins and a 15 mm time column. The minimum readable column is about 25–30 mm, roughly 15–18 characters per line at 7 pt. The type floor follows print guidance: 7 pt is the practical minimum on uncoated stock and 8 pt is the minimum for must-read text ([4over4](https://v2.4over4.com/guide/what-is-the-smallest-font-size-you-can-print)); a government style guide sets 7.8 pt minimum for newsprint ([Tasmanian Gov style guide](https://www.communications.tas.gov.au/styleguide/font/size)).

| Grid page                                           | Usable room width | Rooms at 30 mm | Rooms at 25 mm |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- | -------------- | -------------- |
| A5 portrait (148 mm)                                | ~103 mm           | 3              | 4              |
| A4 portrait / A5 landscape (210 mm)                 | ~165 mm           | 5              | 6              |
| A4 landscape (297 mm)                               | ~252 mm           | 8              | 10             |
| 2 × A4 portrait facing pages (time column repeated) | ~330 mm           | 11             | 13             |
| A3 landscape fold-out (420 mm)                      | ~375 mm           | 12             | 15             |

## Decision matrix

Key: **N = maximum number of rooms in use at the same instant on that day.** Compute it per day so a busy day can switch layout while quiet days stay simple. Breaks and plenaries span all columns and do not count toward N.

| N (per day) | At-a-glance layout                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Grid page format                                                                                           | Cell content                                         |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **≤ 4**     | Rooms as columns, one day per page (or several days per page, as ANR does)                                                                                                                                                                                       | Same as the book: A4 portrait (A5 portrait works up to 3)                                                  | Session title + chair if it fits                     |
| **5–8**     | Rooms as columns, one day per page                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | **Landscape page inside a portrait book** (A4 landscape; in an A5 book, a landscape A5 page up to 5 rooms) | Session code + short title; abbreviated room headers |
| **9–14**    | Rooms as columns **split across a facing-page spread**: rooms 1..k on the left (even) page, k+1..N on the right; time column repeated on both                                                                                                                    | 2 × A4 portrait spread (default), or an A3 landscape fold-out (IDML only)                                  | Code + short title only, 7 pt floor                  |
| **15+**     | **Transposed**: rooms (with their session) as rows; time blocks (session start boundaries, or half-days) as columns; rows grouped by track with a band. Optionally drop the grid and print a per-time-block list ("10:30–12:00: Room A – S12 Title; Room B – …") | Portrait page(s), rows paginate naturally; repeat header row                                               | Session code (+ short title if it fits)              |

Rules that apply at every N:

- Time rows come from the day's **distinct session boundaries**, not from fixed 15-minute ticks. CHI uses 4 slot rows per day. Our sessions have arbitrary `startAt`/`endAt`, so cells use row spans.
- Breaks, events and plenaries are full-width bands.
- Every grid cell needs a pointer into the Detailed program. Options: a generated session code (all surveyed large meetings use codes), or a page reference (TMS 2014). See the open questions.
- Colour by track only with a band or legend label, so the grid still works printed in black and white.

### Per-output notes

**Word (`docx`, already a dependency)**

- Page size and orientation are per **section**, so a landscape grid section between portrait sections is native. Word caps a page at **22 × 22 in** ([MS KB Q95109](https://techshelps.github.io/MSDN/KB/Source/word/q95109.htm)), so A3 (16.5 in) fits and a CHI-style 28 in sheet does not. Tables are capped at **63 columns** ([MS KB 253600](https://ftp.zx.net.nz/pub/archive/ftp.microsoft.com/MISC/KB/en-us/253/600.HTM)), which does not bind here.
- The 9–14 spread has to be **two separate tables**, with the left-page section starting on an even page (section break "even page"). Word has no table that spans a spread.
- A transposed grid (15+) is a normal long table with a repeated header row. It is the easiest layout to make robust in Word.
- An A3 fold-out mixed into an A4 Word book is technically possible, but office printers handle it badly. Recommend the spread instead.

**IDML (InDesign handoff)**

- InDesign supports **mixed page sizes in one document** (since CS5, Page tool), explicitly for gatefolds/fold-outs ([CreativePro](https://creativepro.com/introducing-multiple-page-sizes-in-indesign-cs5/), [Peachpit, Real World InDesign CS5](https://peachpit.com/articles/article.aspx?p=1578514)). An A3 fold-out page is therefore expressible in IDML, but a designer may prefer separate files for print ([CreativePro, same article](https://creativepro.com/introducing-multiple-page-sizes-in-indesign-cs5/)).
- Spreads are first-class in IDML, so the 9–14 split maps to one spread with two tables. Emit the same structure as Word: one table per page plus named table/cell styles. The designer reflows from there.

**xlsx grid sheet**

- No page-width constraint. Always emit **rooms as columns, one sheet per day**, at every N: this is the raw grid organisers sort and edit, and transposing it would hurt that use. Freeze the time column and the header row.
- Print setup (landscape, A3, fit-to-width, repeat titles) only matters if someone prints the sheet. The current library (SheetJS CE `xlsx`) documents only `!margins`, `!cols`, `!rows` and `!merges` at sheet level ([docs](https://docs.sheetjs.com/docs/csf/sheet)). Cell styling (fills for track colours, borders) is listed as a **SheetJS Pro** feature ([sheetjs.com/pro](https://sheetjs.com/pro/)). That bears on the generator choice: coloured or print-ready grids need another library or Pro.

## Our data

- `Room` has `name` + `order`. `ProgramSession` has a nullable `roomId`, `trackId`, `startAt`/`endAt` and `untimedSlots`. `ProgramTrack` has `color`, `series` and `seriesOrder` (`prisma/schema.prisma`).
- **There is no session code field.** Codes would have to be generated at export, or the pointer must be a page reference.
- Rooms have no short name, so abbreviated headers would mean truncation or a new field.
- A session can have `roomId = null`. The grid needs a "no room" column or a fallback.
- The local dev DB has 5 rooms, 3 sessions and at most 2 concurrent, so it says nothing about real scale. The production instance (pjmicro) was not queried.

## Not verified

- Physical print sizes. Whether the CHI sheet was really folded is inferred from PDF dimensions only.
- Exact peak room counts for EGU (conference-wide) and TMS 2025 (rough count from extracted text).
- No current printed ICASSP/ICRA/IROS/CVPR or MRS onsite book exists online; those meetings now ship HTML/app programs.
- A5 European pocket booklets were not found in this sample. A5 limits are derived from the arithmetic only.
- SheetJS CE print setup (orientation, paper size, print titles) is undocumented rather than confirmed absent.
- The capacity table is arithmetic from assumed margins and column minimums, not a sourced standard.
