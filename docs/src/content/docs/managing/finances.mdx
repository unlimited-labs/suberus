The **Finances** screen is a single board where you record what the conference
earns and spends, and watch the **bottom line** update as you type. It is not an
accounting system — think of it as a shared, always-live worksheet for the
organizer. One side lists **expenses**, the other **income**, and a **Simulation**
mode lets you project fee revenue and find your break-even before a single number
is final.

<Aside type="note" title="Where to find it">
**Finances** in the admin sidebar. The screen is **ADMIN-only** — editors don't see it.
It appears only while the feature is enabled on **[Settings › Finances](/settings/finances/)**.
</Aside>

## The board

The screen splits in two: **Expenses** on the left, **Income** on the right. Each
side is a free list of lines you add yourself — there are **no fixed categories**,
because every conference is different. Name a line whatever fits (*Venue*,
*Catering*, *NCN grant*, *Acme sponsorship*).

<Steps>

1. Click **Add expense** / **Add income** to append a line.

2. Type a **name** and an **amount**. All amounts use the conference fee currency
   (set on **[Settings › Fee](/settings/fee/)**).

3. The **Σ Expenses**, **Σ Income** and **Net** totals at the bottom update
   immediately. Net turns **green** when positive, **red** when you're under water.

4. Click **Save** to save the board. Your lines are there next time you open it.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
Nothing is saved until you press **Save** — so you can freely try a line, watch
the net move, and remove it again without touching the stored figures.
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution" title="Unsaved changes are guarded">
If you edit a line and try to leave the page (or close the tab) before saving,
the app warns you so you don't lose the changes. Saving — or confirming the
prompt — clears the warning.
</Aside>

### Export to Excel

**Export XLSX**, under the **Save** button, downloads the current board as a
spreadsheet: every income and expense line (including the collected registration
fees), plus a totals block — **Income**, **Expenses**, and **Net**. The export
reflects the **saved** figures, not an unsaved simulation.

### Registration fees are counted for you

At the top of the income side sits one **read-only** line, **Registration fees** —
the total already collected from paid registration fees. It is computed from the
system, not typed, and you cannot edit or delete it. Everything below it (grants,
sponsorships, anything else) you enter by hand.

## Each line

| Field | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Free text — whatever the line is (no preset category). Required to save. |
| **Contractor** (expenses) | Who you paid. Type a new name, or pick from the suggestions of contractors you've used before — the field remembers past entries so you can reuse them. Optional. |
| **Amount** | A number **or a formula** — type `500` or `2*250`, and the evaluated result (`= 500`) shows next to the field. Supports `+ - * / ( )`, `%`, `^`. The formula text is saved, so it's still there when you reopen. Blank or invalid counts as 0. |
| **✕** | Removes the line. |

### VAT on expenses — Netto / Gross

Each **expense** line has separate **Netto** and **Gross** amount fields plus a
**VAT** rate (from **[Settings › Finances](/settings/finances/)**, or *No VAT*):

- Type into **either** Netto **or** Gross — the other fills in automatically from
  the VAT rate. The field you type in is the source (it keeps your number or
  formula) and shows its evaluated result (`= 1 000`); the other is shown computed
  and slightly greyed.
- Pick the **VAT** rate from the segmented pills (**—** for no VAT, then your
  configured rates). The **VAT amount** (gross − net) shows read-only beside them.
- Formulas work in whichever field you edit; *No VAT* makes Netto = Gross.

Income lines have no VAT — a single **Amount** field.

A **Gross / Net** switch in the **Expenses** header controls whether the
**Expenses** total (and the expense side of **Net**) is summed as gross or net —
so mixing net- and gross-entered costs still totals correctly.

### Status & due date

Each expense card also carries:

- **Ordered** and **Paid** — click the chips to toggle (they light up when on).
- **Due** — an optional due date (`type="date"`).

A **colored left bar** signals status at a glance: **red** when overdue, **green**
when paid, **amber** when ordered but not yet paid. Past-due unpaid expenses also
show a red **"Nd overdue"** badge and tint the card; upcoming ones show a subtle
**"in Nd"** / **"due today"** hint.

These are saved with the expense and included in the Excel export (Ordered / Paid
/ Due columns).

## Simulation

Switch the toggle to **Simulation** to turn the board into a *what-if*. The
read-only registration-fees line is replaced by an editable **fee projection**,
and a **break-even** readout appears under the totals.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Fee projection** | One row per fee type (from **[Settings › Fee](/settings/fee/)**): a **price** and a **quantity**, prefilled from your real prices and the count already paid. Edit either — raise a price, assume more attendees — and the projected income (and net) recompute live. |
| **Break-even** | For each fee type, how many of that fee at its price would cover total expenses given all other income. Shows *never (price ≤ 0)* when the price can't close the gap. |

Simulation is a **throwaway scratchpad**: the projection and its numbers are never
saved. Your recorded expense and income lines stay exactly as you left them.

<Aside type="caution">
Amounts are shown in a single currency — the one set on **[Settings › Fee](/settings/fee/)**.
Finances does not convert between currencies.
</Aside>
