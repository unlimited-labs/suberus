Running a conference means juggling a lot of moving parts — submissions, reviewers,
decisions, emails, and a program to publish. Suberus keeps them in one place, and this
manual walks you through each one in plain language. You don't need to be a developer;
where a technical term is unavoidable, it's explained the first time it comes up.

## Three stages, three sections

The manual follows the shape of a conference, from the empty setup screen to the final
schedule:

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Configuration" icon="setting">
		Get the conference ready — identity, submission types, review rules, emails,
		fees, branding. New here? Start with the **[Quick start](/configuration/quick-start/)**.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Managing the conference" icon="open-book">
		The day-to-day once submissions start arriving: assign reviewers, make decisions,
		and look after your people. Begin with the **[Quick start](/managing/quick-start/)**.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Program Planner" icon="rocket">
		Turn accepted talks into a timetable and publish it — by hand or with the
		autoplanner. Start from the **[Overview](/planner/overview/)**.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## Finding your way around

Most pages walk you through a task step by step, then list every field on the screen —
what it does, what to put there, and anything worth watching out for. The screenshots
come from a sample conference ("ICCMS 2026"), so you can line them up against your own
installation as you go — click any screenshot to open it full-screen.
