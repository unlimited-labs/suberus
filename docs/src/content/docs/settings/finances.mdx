The **Finances** tab holds a single switch that controls whether the
**[Finances](/managing/finances/)** screen is available in the admin menu.

<Aside type="note" title="Where to find it">
**[Settings](/admin/settings/) › Finances**.
</Aside>

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Finances enabled** | **Off by default.** When on, **Finances** appears in the admin sidebar (ADMIN only). When off, the screen is hidden from the menu. Your saved figures are kept either way. |

🖼️ *[Screenshot placeholder — Settings › Finances tab with the enable switch]*

## VAT rates

While Finances is enabled, a **VAT rates** table appears below the switch. These
are the rates offered when you enter a cost as **net** or **gross** on the
Finances board. Two rates ship by default — **8%** and **23%** — and you can add
or remove your own (any whole number 0–100).

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Add** | Type a rate (%) and click **Add** to append it to the list. |
| **✕** | Removes a rate. Existing saved costs keep the rate already stored on them. |
